The proof contract

Show pass conditions on screen

The verifier—not the presenter—should make the claims. Final verification uses exported files only and requires no trust in the gateway, plugin process, ledger service, or exporter.

01 · Fidelity

One finalized record per invocation

Allow, deny, modified, DenyIgnored, Aborted, and plugin panic retain distinct semantics.

02 · Completeness

Dense sequence within each epoch and stream

A stream_seq gap is evidence of loss. emission_seq establishes ordering only.

03 · Record integrity

AID-EMIT-1 fingerprint reproduces

Strip only the declared derived fields, JCS-canonicalize, hash with SHA-256, and compare.

04 · Origin

DSSE signature verifies offline

ECDSA P-256 signs the DSSE PAE of the same canonical bytes covered by the fingerprint.

05 · Durability

Ledger V3 chains verify

The ledger independently binds stored content, metadata, and chain position after ingestion.

06 · Portability

PPE requires a dependency swap only

After Policy #11 lands, rerun the same plugin protocol. Any plugin source change is a port finding.

Ownership

Three projects, three sign-offs

The split deliberately follows the architecture. Nobody needs to own another project’s implementation to verify the result.

Teryl · PPE / CPEX

Own the deterministic enforcement traffic, effect interruption, recovery, stream-stamp generation, and eventual Policy #11 port.

  • Verdict fidelity
  • Awaited audit-handler contract
  • Generate epoch and sequence stamps
  • Effect recovery semantics
  • Zero plugin-source changes on PPE

Jeff · OCSF / AID-EMIT-1

Own the record mapping, covered bytes, deterministic signing, binding CPEX’s stream stamps into the hashed bytes, and the cross-host conformance report.

  • OCSF vocabulary
  • JCS fingerprint reproduction
  • DSSE verification
  • Bind stamps into the fingerprint
  • Stream semantics and CPEX↔PPE comparison

Jonathan · Immutable ledger

Own ingestion, durable storage, epoch-aware gap detection, receipts, queries, and the repeatable demo runner.

  • No current-shape records skipped
  • V3 chain verification
  • Conversation and agent queries
  • Request-key retention
  • One-command reproducibility
